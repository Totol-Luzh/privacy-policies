Privacy Policy

Light Canvas
Effective date: November 5, 2026
Package: com.bytewave.lightcanvas

Light Canvas ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application for controlling a DIY LED matrix over Bluetooth. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal data. You do not need to create an account or sign in to use the app. All settings you change (brightness, speed, selected effect, saved color, etc.) are stored locally on your device only using Android DataStore, and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Permissions and How They Are Used

The app requests only the following Android permissions, and uses them solely for the purposes described below:

  • Bluetooth Connect (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT on Android 12+, BLUETOOTH on Android 11 and below) — used to list already paired Bluetooth devices, connect to the LED matrix you select, and send commands to it. The app does not scan for nearby Bluetooth devices.
  • Photo / Media access — used only when you explicitly pick an image from your gallery to display on the matrix. The image is processed locally (cropped and scaled) and sent to your device. We do not upload your photos to any server.

3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, etc.).

4. Data Storage and Deletion

All data (settings, MAC address of the last connected device) is stored exclusively in your phone's local storage. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

5. Security

We value your trust, but please remember that no method of transmission over Bluetooth or storage on a device is 100% secure. We recommend using standard security measures on your phone.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with any information, please contact us, and we will take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version takes effect as soon as it is published on this page. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
